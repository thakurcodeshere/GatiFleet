Digital Twin Architecture

GatiFleet represents physical and organizational assets (Customers, Vehicles, Drivers) as active Digital Twins. Rather than storing flat status columns, a Digital Twin maintains multiple state machines and cognitive "brains" that simulate financial and operational outcomes.

Overview of the Six Brains

Each customer's digital twin is split into six cognitive brains to process metrics concurrently:

1. Commercial Brain

Simulates customer contract lifetimes (LTV), margin protection thresholds, volume commitments, and active RFQ proposal conversions. It generates warnings if a customer's contract margin falls below corporate limits.

2. Operational Brain

Parses live telematics, checkpost updates, and geofence events. It continuously calculates the On-Time Delivery (OTD) index and SLA penalty exposures, requesting route adjustments from the planning engine if delays occur.

3. Financial Brain

Manages accounts receivables (AR) aging parameters, dispute triggers, toll validations, and fuel surcharge updates. It calculates working capital requirements for customer corridors.

4. Relationship Brain

Evaluates support ticket counts, case resolution speeds, and helpdesk chat logs using sentiment analysis to calculate an overall Relationship Index (0-100%).

5. Strategic Brain

Tracks shipment patterns and matches them against market capacity, suggesting expansion corridor paths or warning account teams of competitive risks.

6. Learning Brain

Compares estimated trip durations, fuel surcharges, and asset utilization metrics against actual results, retraining heuristics to reduce planning errors.
