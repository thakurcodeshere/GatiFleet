Module 12 — Autonomous Financial Intelligence & Treasury Platform (AFITP)

The Autonomous Financial Intelligence & Treasury Platform (AFITP) manages GatiFleet's real-time financial digital twin, checking P&L status across modules.

1. Business Goal

Maintain treasury liquidity and protect margins by automatically mapping operational logistics transactions directly to P&L reports.

2. Core Components

  • Financial Digital Twin: Models bank balances, accounts payable/receivable (AP/AR), and working capital limits in real-time.
  • Real-Time Margin Tracker: Computes contribution margins at customer, corridor, vehicle, and driver levels.
  • Collections Risk Solver: Evaluates invoice aging limits to forecast working capital trends.

3. Financial Transaction Mapping

flowchart LR
    Event[L1 Operational Event] --> Accrual[Accrual Calculator]
    Accrual --> PnL[Real-time P&L Ledger]
    PnL --> Cash[Treasury Forecast]
    Event -->|FASTag / Fuel Surcharge| Accrual

4. Event Taxonomy

Event Name Trigger Database / State Changes
TRANSACTION_ACCRUED FASTag crossing or driver allowance logged. Creates accrual ledger row; recalculates margin metrics.
COLLECTIONS_ALERT_TRIGGERED AR invoice exceeds payment days limit. Updates customer risk scores; flags profile.
TREASURY_RECONCILIATION_COMPLETED Bank statement matched to AR ledger. Updates invoice status to 'Settled'; frees credit lines.
