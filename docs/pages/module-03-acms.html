Module 3 — Autonomous Customer Mobilization System (ACMS)

The Autonomous Customer Mobilization System (ACMS) coordinates the operational setup and compliance validation of a new customer immediately after a deal is marked won.

1. Business Goal

Ensure 100% operational readiness across commercial, compliance, technology, and fleet lanes before launching the first commercial shipment, minimizing onboarding delays and SLA defaults.

2. Onboarding lanes

On mobilization startup, ACMS parallelizes workflows across 9 distinct lanes:

  1. Customer Setup: Maps client organizational hierarchy and plant locations.
  2. Commercial Setup: Parses and structures contract lane tariffs, billing intervals, and penalties.
  3. Finance Setup: Sets accounts receivable limits, credit thresholds, and toll surcharge rules.
  4. Fleet Setup: Reserves dedicated tractors and runs telematics/ADAS checkups.
  5. Driver Setup: Roster familiar drivers and verifies safety safety logs.
  6. Technology Setup: Configures EDI pipelines and generates Client Portal credentials.
  7. Support Setup: Configures helpdesk severity SLA matrices.
  8. Executive Setup: Assigns GatiFleet Account Directors to customer counterpart sponsors.
  9. Compliance Setup: Verifies FASTag permits, regional state permits, and carrier insurance.

    ScoreCheck -->|No| Fix[Address Onboarding Bottlenecks]
    Fix --> Simulation

4. Event Taxonomy

Event Name Trigger Database / State Changes
CUSTOMER_MOBILIZATION_STARTED Opportunity status is updated to 'Closed Won'. Creates Onboarding task logs; sets Customer status to 'Mobilizing'.
READINESS_SIMULATION_COMPLETED Mock transaction passes compliance and telematics checks. Calculates final readiness percentage.
CUSTOMER_FULLY_OPERATIONAL Readiness score matches or exceeds the 90% threshold. Sets Customer status to 'Active', opens booking portals.
