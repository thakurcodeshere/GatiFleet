Module 4 — Autonomous Commercial Agreement Intelligence System (ACAIS)

The Autonomous Commercial Agreement Intelligence System (ACAIS) translates raw legal agreements into executable digital policy parameters, monitoring SLA violation risks in real-time.

1. Business Goal

Protect operating margins and avoid contractual penalties by parsing legal clauses into structured code rules and forecasting renewability months before expiration.

2. System Mechanics

  • Clause Compiler: Runs regex and OCR parsers on contract PDFs to extract rate escalators, volume commitments, and SLA thresholds. Converts them to executable logic code blocks (e.g. IF Transit_Time > 72 HOURS THEN Trigger_Penalty(5%)).
  • Commercial Risk Index: Calculates exposure risk (0-100) based on historical route volatility, fuel price indicators, and asset locking expenses.
  • SLA Monitor: Regularly checks shipment transit profiles, warning the dispatch panel if a route is trending toward an SLA penalty event.

4. Event Taxonomy

Event Name Trigger Database / State Changes
AGREEMENT_COMPILED Legal team uploads signed PDF. Inserts contract parameters; compiles active logic policies.
SLA_WARN_TRIGGERED Active trip ETA matches or exceeds contract SLA limit. Flags trip record; dispatches re-routing requests to Dispatch.
RENEWAL_FORECAST_GENERATED Contract hits its 90-day pre-expiry window. Calculates estimated margin outcome; updates pipeline.
