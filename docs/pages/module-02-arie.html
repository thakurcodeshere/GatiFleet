Module 2 — Autonomous Revenue Intelligence Engine (ARIE)

The Autonomous Revenue Intelligence Engine (ARIE) oversees the sales pipeline, lane viability solvers, and real-time quoting algorithms.

1. Business Goal

Optimize gross margin and win probabilities by executing logistics feasibility checks and matching competitor pricing before committing to a contract lane tariff.

2. Core Components

  • Logistics Feasibility Solver: Scans regional depot capacity, equipment availability, and border clearance queues to determine route viability.
  • AI Pricing Engine: Formulates three distinct pricing options:
    1. Best Price: Balances competitive bids with optimal win probability.
    2. Best Margin: Sets peak tariff pricing matching seasonal corridor demand density.
    3. Negotiation Range: Defines the absolute bottom floor tariff to guard trip operating costs.
  • Buying Organization Graph: Maps the client procurement tree (CEO, procurement heads, warehouse contacts) with sentiment tracking.

4. Event Taxonomy

Event Name Trigger Database / State Changes
OPPORTUNITY_REQUESTED Sales operator requests new quote template. Creates a pending Opportunity row; locks temporary capacity reservation.
FEASIBILITY_VERIFIED Route and asset check solver successfully completes. Updates Opportunity feasibility status; saves estimated corridor route.
PROPOSAL_GENERATED Pricing engine produces the three tariff options. Attaches pricing ranges to Opportunity record.
