Module 5 — Revenue Assurance & Autonomous Billing Intelligence Platform (RABIP)

The Revenue Assurance & Autonomous Billing Intelligence Platform (RABIP) matches operational events (telemetry, FASTag crossings, sensor logs) against commercial contract rules to automate invoicing and invoice validation.

1. Business Goal

Eliminate revenue leakage and billing disputes by dynamically calculating freight ratings, fuel surcharges, accessorial delays, and SLA penalties without manual intervention.

2. Core Modules

  • Revenue Event Ledger: Ingests GPS locations, geo-fence transitions, and loading dock timestamps as raw billing events.
  • Fraud & Override Scanner: Cross-checks driver manual overrides and repeated toll reimbursement claims, flagging anomalous records with a fraud score.
  • Dynamic Fuel Rating Engine: Automatically adjusts fuel surcharge adjustments based on regional daily fuel indexes.
  • SLA Penalty Adjuster: Applies penalties or rebates automatically to invoice lines based on transit logs.

3. Billing Lifecycle Flow

stateDiagram-v2
    [*] --> PendingRating : Trip Completed
    PendingRating --> RatingCalculated : Apply Surcharge & Accessorials
    RatingCalculated --> AuditFailed : Fraud / GPS Mismatch
    RatingCalculated --> Audited : Validation Checked
    Audited --> InvoiceGenerated : Create Legal PDF
    InvoiceGenerated --> [*] : Invoiced

4. Event Taxonomy

Event Name Trigger Database / State Changes
BILLABLE_EVENT_NORMALIZED GPS telemetry shows vehicle entering customer geofence. Logs normalized timestamp, corridor, and GPS signature.
INVOICE_AUDIT_COMPLETED Fraud check matches toll crossings against logs. Saves fraud score; updates status to 'Approved' or 'Flagged'.
INVOICE_DISPATCHED Invoice JSON compiles and registers. Applies P&L values to financial ledgers; emails client.
