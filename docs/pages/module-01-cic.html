Module 1 — Customer Intelligence Core (CIC)

The Customer Intelligence Core (CIC) maps and monitors the strategic, commercial, and operational status of shippers and organizational clients in GatiFleet.

1. Business Goal

Maintain a unified digital twin of the customer's operational and financial interactions to support high-accuracy risk forecasts (such as churn or delay risks) and optimize pricing tiers.

2. Core Entity Aggregate Model

A customer is represented as a connected aggregate root in the database, containing references to:

  • Organization: Legal name, registration number, GSTIN, and business segment.
  • Contacts: Corporate sponsors, billing contacts, and site operations managers.
  • Contracts: Signed agreements, pricing formulas, and SLA parameters.
  • Digital Twin: Six-dimensional cognitive brains tracking metrics in real-time.

3. Customer Digital Twin brains

Each customer profile maintains six distinct simulated "brains":

  1. Commercial Brain: Tracks LTV, gross margin, active pipeline value, and contract renewal dates.
  2. Operational Brain: Monitored shipment counts, on-time delivery rates, and SLA penalty exposures.
  3. Financial Brain: Tracks outstanding accounts receivables (AR), aging invoices, and toll/fuel surcharges.
  4. Relationship Brain: Records executive cadences, support ticket sentiment, and NPS ratings.
  5. Strategic Brain: Explores lane growth potential, competitive threats, and corridor options.
  6. Learning Brain: Analyzes divergence between operational estimates and real-world outcomes.

4. Customer State Machine

stateDiagram-v2
    [*] --> Prospect
    Prospect --> Qualified : Details Verified
    Qualified --> Opportunity : RFQ Created
    Opportunity --> Contracted : Deal Won
    Contracted --> Active : First Load Booked
    Active --> Expanding : Volume Increase
    Active --> AtRisk : SLA Drops / Late Payments
    AtRisk --> Recovering : Resolution Met
    AtRisk --> Lost : Inactive > 90 Days

5. Event Taxonomy

Event Name Trigger Database / State Changes
CUSTOMER_CREATED New profile saved manually or imported from ERP. Creates Organization row; initial digital twin state is set.
HEALTH_RECALCULATED SLA status changes or new payment delay logged. Recalculates multi-dimensional weighted health index.
CUSTOMER_AT_RISK Overall health score drops below threshold (e.g. 50%). Sets status to 'At Risk', dispatches alerts to account manager.
