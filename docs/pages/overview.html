GatiFleet OS Overview

GatiFleet v1.0 Preview. This documentation represents the specification framework for GatiFleet, India's next-generation enterprise Operating System for transportation and logistics. Expect progressive additions as new neural agents are integrated.

GatiFleet is a high-performance, next-generation enterprise Operating System designed from the ground up for the Indian transportation ecosystem. Built for modern fleet operators and large-scale shippers, GatiFleet unifies real-time telematics, automated dispatching, driver payroll, and business logic layers into a single, cohesive interface.

Every operational transaction within GatiFleet is modeled through an Event-Driven Architecture (EDA). No button directly mutates data. Clicking, scheduling, or re-routing dispatches a structured event payload, which propagates downline to feed digital twin simulations, retraining modules, and ERP ledgers.

Key Architecture Pillars

Event-Driven Core

Every action dispatches L1 business events to a central ledger, keeping intelligence and operational layers synchronized.

Digital Twin Simulation

Every customer, vehicle, and driver maintains a digital twin that simulates financial, operational, and physical outcomes.

Self-Learning Heuristics

Continuous feedback loops validate AI recommendations against real-world operations to retrain dispatch solvers.

Core Intelligence Modules

GatiFleet partitions its system operations into 12 primary modules, mapping to distinct business layers:

  • CIC (Customer Intelligence Core): Tracks strategic metrics, LTV, contracts, and core organizational relationships.
  • ARIE (Revenue Intelligence): Executes routing viability checks, competitor bids mapping, and tier pricing optimization.
  • ACMS (Customer Mobilization): Orchestrates post-sale handoff, SSO setups, and FASTag permit compliance.
  • ACAIS (Commercial Agreement Intelligence): Evaluates contract SLA exposure limits and triggers margins warnings.
  • RABIP (Revenue Assurance & Billing): Matches FASTag crossings and telemetry inputs against contract clauses.
  • ACOIP (Customer Operations): Analyzes weather and lane congestion to trigger proactive incident resolutions.
  • IFAOS (Fleet & Asset Operations): Ingests vehicle telemetry to model Remaining Useful Life (RUL) on components.
  • ATPDS (Transport Planning & Dispatch): Evaluates routes against empty kilometers, fuel indexes, and driver rest states.
  • DWIP (Driver Workforce Intelligence): Manages driver rosters, fatigue tracking, rest hours, and compliance records.
  • LIIS (Logistics Infrastructure): Tracks warehouse racking allocations, forklift GPS, and yard constraints.
  • ASNIP (Supply Network Intelligence): Runs multi-modal ocean/rail/road solvers to bypass regional bottlenecks.
  • AFITP (Financial Intelligence & Treasury): Computes contribution margins in real-time at trip and corridor levels.
