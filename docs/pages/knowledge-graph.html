Knowledge Graph Synchronization

Every operational transaction within GatiFleet updates a centralized Supply Chain Knowledge Graph. This represents live spatial, commercial, and operational dependencies, allowing the AI agents to locate bottlenecks and trace root causes.

Graph Relationship Model

The knowledge graph connects entities using semantic edges. A simplified layout of relationships is mapped below:

graph LR
    CUS[Customer] -->|HAS_CONTRACT| CON[Contract]
    CUS -->|USES_ROUTE| RTE[Route Corridor]
    CUS -->|CREATES_CASES| CAS[Support Case]
    RTE -->|SERVED_BY| FLT[Fleet Vehicle]
    FLT -->|ASSIGNED_TO| DRV[Driver Profile]
    RTE -->|DEPENDS_ON| WH[Warehouse Hub]

Core Graph Node Types

  • Customer: Holds organizational attributes, digital twin references, and global health ratings.
  • Contract: Defines SLA penalty rules, corridor tariffs, and payment days.
  • Route Corridor: Tracks checkpoints, FASTag toll coordinates, and average transit durations.
  • Fleet Vehicle: Holds engine metrics, tyre health configurations, and trailer dimensions.
  • Driver Profile: Records compliance ratings, rest logs, and lane safety scores.
  • Warehouse Hub: Represents yard layouts, scale capacities, and labor schedules.

Dynamic Event Sync

Unlike relational databases that perform nightly batch updates, GatiFleet syncs graph relationships immediately on L1 events:

  1. CUSTOMER_CREATED: Inserts a new Customer node and maps relations to Assigned Account Managers.
  2. DISPATCH_COMMITTED: Establishes a temporary SERVES edge between a Fleet Vehicle, Driver, and the Customer node.
  3. ROUTE_DEVIATION_ALERT: Appends a temporary BLOCKED_BY or DEVIATED_FROM edge, triggering a route re-calculation check.
